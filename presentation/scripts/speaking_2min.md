# 2-Minute Speaking Version

UniAdmit is our undergraduate final-year project focused on digitizing the admission process end-to-end. Instead of manual, fragmented workflows, we provide a centralized web platform where students can register with OTP verification, submit applications, pay fees, and track their status.

The system is built using Django with two primary apps: `core` for admission workflows and `chatbot` for AI-based applicant support. Admin users can review applications, manage admission rounds, publish merit list PDFs, and export applicant data.

A key differentiator is the integrated chatbot. It answers admission queries from a managed knowledge base, records unanswered questions, and accepts feedback so responses can continuously improve.

Overall, the project improves transparency, reduces administrative overhead, and gives students faster guidance during admission. It is practical, extensible, and ready for future enhancements like analytics, notifications, and stronger AI grounding.
